The thesis

A name that states the loan.

Most lender brands sound like banks or generic mortgage shops. ShortStayLoans.com sounds like a product: loans for properties rented by the night, the week, and the season.

That bluntness is the point. An investor deciding where to finance a vacation rental does not want to decode a brand. The investor wants to know, before anything else, that the lender on the other side of the screen underwrites short-stay income — nightly rates, occupancy, seasonality — instead of forcing it into a long-term lease box.

The name is pronounceable, exact, and search-shaped: it matches the words an investor types when the conventional route has just said no to projected rental income. It works on a rate page, on a loan calculator, on a broker's email signature, and in front of an investor who needs to know, in one glance, that this is not a general mortgage site with a vacation-rental page bolted on. It is a short-stay lender first.

Specific, and broad enough to travel. A strong short-stay lending brand must do three things at once: attract borrowers, reassure underwriters, and travel across markets. This name is specific enough to rank in the mind of an investor searching for short-stay financing, and broad enough to cover DSCR loans, vacation-rental purchase and refinance, portfolio lending, and a marketplace matching borrowers with STR lenders from the coast to the mountains. It avoids jargon that only one program understands, and it avoids vagueness that says nothing at all.
