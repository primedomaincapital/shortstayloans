Where short-stay loans and rental data actually live.

Last checked: November 2, 2026

A short-stay loan sits at the meeting point of two markets: the rental market, where nightly income is earned, and the lending market, where that income is underwritten. The links below cover both sides — the data that measures the income, the lenders that finance against it, the guides that explain the underwriting, and the platforms where the stays are booked.

Market data and research

AirDNA Blog

Short-term rental market analysis, occupancy and rate trends, and industry reports.

AirDNA, U.S. Review December 2025

Full-year 2025 U.S. occupancy of 56.9%, the December 2025 snapshot (51.0% occupancy, $248.57 ADR, $126.97 RevPAR), and supply growth at its weakest recorded rate in July 2025.

Phocuswright, Global Short-Term Rentals 2026 summary

Global gross bookings of $219.9 billion in 2025, projected to $270.6 billion by 2029, with North America at $78 billion in 2025.

Grand View Research, U.S. short-term vacation rental outlook

U.S. revenue of $43,857.5 million in 2025, projected to $100,107.8 million by 2033, with homes the largest accommodation type at 45.51% of revenue.
